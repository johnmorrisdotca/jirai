THE RULES ARE SMALL

Read. Mark. Uncover.

  1. Open a cell. A number tells you how many neighbouring cells hide a mine. Empty ground opens out on its own.
  2. Mark what you know. Right-click, hold, or choose Mark. Flags are notes, not proof; placing one doesn’t change the board.
  3. Clear the field. Open every cell without a mine to finish. When flags match a revealed number, tap it to open the neighbours together.

A fair deduction

No-guess fields are verified from the opening you choose. The mines stay fixed after that.
